# Proper Infinite Horizons — Mindful Salvage Possibility

## Scope

This is not a core progression pillar and the pack should not be balanced around speedrunning.

The intended experience remains a long-form industrial sandbox in which players build, repair, automate, and expand at their own pace. However, the world should occasionally create unusually favorable combinations of structures, machine remnants, materials, and salvage.

A highly attentive player may notice that combination and act on it quickly.

## Mindfulness, not a guaranteed route

The interesting possibility is not simply that a lucky chest can skip progression. It is that the player remains alert enough to recognize when the world has quietly presented a shortcut.

That awareness may involve:

- recognizing a useful machine line from its shape;
- noticing that only one pipe, shaft, belt, or cogwheel is missing;
- realizing that a recovered component bypasses a difficult crafting bottleneck;
- choosing to repair a ruin instead of dismantling it;
- identifying complementary structures before committing to a permanent base;
- keeping seemingly unrelated parts because they may complete a later machine;
- abandoning a structure immediately when it does not support the emerging route.

Luck creates an opportunity. Mindfulness is noticing what the opportunity actually is.

## Rare compression

In exceptional circumstances, several independent opportunities might align closely enough that an expert player can reach extremely advanced crafting within roughly an hour.

That should remain:

- possible rather than promised;
- emergent rather than scripted;
- dependent on knowledge and attention rather than one jackpot chest;
- irrelevant to how an ordinary playthrough is paced;
- rare enough that discovering such a route feels surprising.

The pack does not need to guarantee that every seed supports this. It only needs to avoid unnecessary restrictions that would invalidate a legitimate route when the world happens to produce one.

## Structure support

Custom structures can support this possibility naturally by containing:

- visible but damaged processing lines;
- missing conveyor spans;
- absent shafts or cogwheels;
- severed fluid pipes;
- incomplete power connections;
- partly assembled multiblocks;
- intact individual machines surrounded by broken infrastructure;
- material stores that suggest what the site once produced;
- specialized salvage pools tied to the structure's former purpose.

Structures should still be useful and understandable during ordinary play. They must not exist only as speedrun tools.

## Restraint

Do not balance normal progression around the assumption that players will find rare salvage.

Do not place complete endgame chains in a single chest or ruin.

Do not make crafting irrelevant by flooding the world with advanced parts.

Do not turn the one-hour possibility into an achievement requirement, quest objective, promised seed frequency, or central marketing claim.

The correct feeling is simply:

> I almost walked past that. Then I realized what all these pieces could become.
